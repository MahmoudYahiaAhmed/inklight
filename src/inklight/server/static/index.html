    Scope: all documents
    Each value is read at a printed label of that name (dates, amounts and ids are typed) and keeps its page and box. Raw extraction: review before use.
    Searches the text of every document (Arabic and English). Click a result to open its page.
    Review covers named fields: Inklight proposes each value from a printed label of that name (or a typed field such as a date or total) and you accept, correct or mark it absent. It is not a correction of the whole document's text. The original reading is kept next to your decision, and only approved reviews are exported.
